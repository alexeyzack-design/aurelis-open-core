# Funding strategy

Target channels:
- Bitcoin open-source grants
- Local-first AI grants
- Bitcoin/AI developer bounties
- Open-source paid tasks
- Hackathons and technical competitions

Every opportunity must record: source URL, date checked, requirements, payout, deadline, eligibility, application status and payment proof.

Success is only recorded as PAID after an on-chain transaction is independently verified.

A credible application must identify a concrete public Bitcoin ecosystem benefit, publish under a verified OSI-approved license, and request milestone-based support. Do not imply it improves Bitcoin security or production wallet safety unless demonstrated.

### Decision and safety rules
- Do not pay an application fee, deposit funds, connect a wallet to an unverified bounty site, or share private keys/seed phrases.
- Do not record an opportunity as APPLIED unless an application was actually submitted; do not record AWARDED without written award confirmation; do not record PAID without independently verified settlement.
- Funding targets are uncertain. Prioritize work that produces a public artifact and a credible paid deliverable in parallel; no grant or job above is guaranteed.
